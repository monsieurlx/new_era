4. Incorporate Fibonacci Retracements

The most common Fibonacci ratios used in trading are 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, and 100%. These ratios can be used to find entry and exit points for trades, as well as to set stop-loss and take-profit orders.  
Fibonacci retracements can also be used in conjunction with other technical indicators to confirm potential support and resistance levels.
While Fibonacci retracements are a popular tool among traders, it’s important to remember that they are not exact and that prices can sometimes move outside of the expected levels.
